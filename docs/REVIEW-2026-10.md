## Fixes in the final pass

- `swfLoading.test.ts` read its fixture with `new URL(..., import.meta.url)`, which Vite rewrites as an asset URL under test. It now resolves a file path like `swfManifest.test.ts` does and uses `CANONICAL_SWF.bytes` instead of a raw size. No hash, ROM or assertion was changed.
- Ruffle loading of the verified bytes (`data` + `allowScriptAccess: false`) was confirmed in the browser: canvas visible, no console or CSP error, altered SWF rejected.
- `scripts/build-reconstruction-manifest.mjs` pointed at a deleted file (`src/game/audio.ts`) and two wrong owners. Fixed in the generator, then the manifest was regenerated. The manifest test now checks that each owner file exists and declares its export.
- Documentation updated: README, CONTRIBUTING, ARCHITECTURE, OWNERSHIP, CODEOWNERS, PR template, Sonar paths. `SECURITY.md` added.
- **Phone landscape framing (844×390 and 667×375):** tuned landscape camera target to `[0, 0.45, 0.35]` and `landscapeDistanceScale` to `1.34` in `src/config/presentation.json`. Compact `SHORT_LANDSCAPE` styling applied to `AdventureHud` and `BottomBar`. The entire cabinet, all 9 holes, Pink, and the birthday cake now sit cleanly above the bottom bar. Verified with visual screenshots and automated in `cameraComposition.test.ts`.
- **3D mesh tap alignment:** added `tapRaycastAlignment.test.ts` testing Three.js raycasting for all 9 holes across 5 viewports (phone portrait, tablet portrait, desktop, phone landscape, small phone landscape), verifying center and ±25% offset taps hit the correct hole. Direct canvas clicks are also exercised in Playwright E2E.
- **Full campaign execution:** added an automated test in `GameSession.test.ts` playing through the complete 6-mission adventure with GameSession event flushing, presenter routing, upgrade selection, and final victory.

# Paingpaing’s Adventure

The default mode is a six-mission adventure. Winning objectives have been doubled: 16 Goonks, 16 singers, 12 first-boss damage, 12 impostors, 16 helmets and 24 final-boss damage. Classic is available from the welcome screen and end-of-run panel. Upgrades reset on a new run; health and continue credits remain finite.

## Five gameplay changes

1. **Tactical donut.** Resting advances the real eating animation. Four bites consume an entire donut, charge sugar and trigger a short frenzy. Striking interrupts eating for the flight. The Sugar Rush button spends four charges to freeze normal Goonks and double points for six seconds.
2. **Goonks cooperate.** Thieves steal sugar and coins, blue-hat tinkerers give friends porcelain helmets, and jesters and their partners look at each other and throw large 3D bananas before swapping. The warning lasts 1.5 seconds. After the swap lands, the jester runs to Pink, snatches the equipped weapon with a STOLEN tag, and sprints back to its hole. The player cannot strike until tapping the carrier; Pink then flies over, slaps the thief, and takes the weapon back. If the carrier escapes, the weapon stays on the board for pickup. Each plush belongs to only one announced pair at a time. A broken helmet leaves its owner frightened and crying.
3. **Different objectives.** Protect a birthday cake, silence bell-wearing singers, identify moustached impostors while leaving green bow ties alone, and break helmets. Escapes, noise, wrong guesses, time and empty hearts can end a mission.
4. **Build an adventure.** Each completed nonfinal mission restores eight health and offers three distinct unowned upgrades. Nine powers support combinations: faster eating, a bouncing mallet, a single-target crystal ray, recovery/guard, longer frenzy, a gold hammer, more time, cake insurance and a healing score jackpot. Ray ammo refills to 50 per round after acquiring the ray.
5. **Boss arenas.** Missions three and six replace the carpet with an actual perforated metal concert deck, neon rails, speakers and an animated equalizer. A giant boss occupies three weak-point lanes, telegraphs attacks in red and exposes a gold crystal on its head or fists. Missed windows produce a table-wide shock wave. Small Goonks also occupy the six free holes. The first boss wears a royal gold crown; the larger final boss has a horned helmet, pink mohawk, dark cape, metal shoulders and spiked cuffs.

## Presentation and controls

Tap any hole or use the numpad. Tap Sugar Rush when all four cells are charged. Pause and sound controls remain in the footer. The objective and charge panel reserve their own screen space; the pink companion stays below the board between strikes.

New arcade effects include spring arrivals, curved swap jumps, a sparkling flight trail, crystal impact bursts, expanding shock rings and an orbiting sugar storm. After a swap, the thief dashes to Pink and back with the stolen weapon. Recovering it plays Pink’s slap flight. Impacts have no comic lettering or full-screen flashes. Reduced-motion preferences disable intense motion: banana throws keep a lower arc and do not spin, and the steal run snaps instead of sprinting. Particle trails and sugar storms use instancing.

## Verification

Automated checks cover full adventures with five deterministic seeds, individual objectives and failure states, upgrade combinations, boss timing, helper isolation, pause/continue/reset, single-target laser ammo, the complete donut cycle, and ray tests proving all nine holes remain open in the boss deck.
